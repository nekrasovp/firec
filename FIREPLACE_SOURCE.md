# Fireplace source

The embedded ASCII animation is derived from
[`Fantastic-fireplace-fire-chimney-hearth-background-texture-motion-graphics-free-video-library.webm`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fantastic-fireplace-fire-chimney-hearth-_background_-_texture_-_motion_graphics_-_free_video_library.webm)
by **free video library**. The source is licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

An eight-second excerpt of the 1920x1080 source is sampled at 12 FPS, resized
to 128x72, and encoded in a compact warm-color R4G3B1 format by
`generate_fireplace_frames.py`. It is rendered with terminal truecolor. The
generated frames are a modified version of the source work.
